# Vietnamese style and glossary (2026-11-02)

Rules every Vietnamese string in MUSQ follows: the coaching copy in
`Content.strings`, the UI in `Localizable.xcstrings`, and any later addition.
The owner picked: everything Vietnamese except **exercise names, which stay
English everywhere**, and muscle names in **Vietnamese anatomical** form.

## Rules

1. **Exercise names stay English, character for character** — every name in
   `exercise_names.txt` (and the lift's own name wherever it appears in a
   sentence: "unlike the Romanian Deadlift, …" → "khác với Romanian Deadlift, …").
   Lift nouns Vietnamese lifters use as-is may stay English in lower case:
   squat, deadlift, lunge, plank, push-up, pull-up, chin-up, dip, hip thrust,
   crunch, burpee. Movement verbs are translated: press → đẩy, row → kéo,
   curl → cuốn, raise → nâng, fly → ép (chest) / dang tay (rear delt),
   extension → duỗi, pulldown → kéo xuống, pushdown → ấn xuống, shrug → nhún vai,
   carry → mang tạ đi.
2. **Tone**: clear, natural Vietnamese a coach would say. Instructions are
   imperatives without a pronoun ("Giữ cổ tay thẳng"); use "bạn" only where a
   sentence needs a subject. No slang, no added advice, nothing left out.
3. **Keep** numbers, °, %, ~, ×, "1RM", "RIR", "RPE", "EMG", "MVIC", "MUSQ",
   "Premium", "Preset" and author/study citations exactly. Units: cm, kg, lb
   stay; time words are translated (3 s → 3 giây, 2 min → 2 phút).
4. **Case**: a string in UPPER CASE stays upper case in Vietnamese
   ("CORRECT FORM" → "TƯ THẾ ĐÚNG"); Title Case names become sentence case
   ("Middle Pectoralis" → "Cơ ngực lớn (phần giữa)"); lower-case lists stay
   lower case.
5. **Length**: on-model labels, cue titles, badges, chips and buttons are read
   at a glance and sit in fixed spaces — keep them about as long as the English
   (labels at most ~25% longer). Prose may be as long as natural Vietnamese needs.
6. Spelling: "khuỷu tay", "xương bả vai", "cẳng tay", "cổ tay", "đầu gối" or
   "gối", "thắt lưng", "hông".
