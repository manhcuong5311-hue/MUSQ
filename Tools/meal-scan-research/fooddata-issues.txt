=== MISSING ===
- MyFitnessPal acquired Cal AI; the deal closed in December 2025 and was announced on 2026-03-02. Cal AI stays a separate app but now uses MyFitnessPal's nutrition database (about 20 million foods, 68,500 brands, 380+ restaurant chains). Cal AI then had over 15M downloads and over $30M in annual revenue. This updates the 2025 picture of Cal AI using open-source databases.
  src: https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/02/myfitnesspal-has-acquired-cal-ai-the-viral-calorie-app-built-by-teens/ (2026-03-02)

- An NIDDK study presented at NUTRITION 2026 tested four photo apps (MyFitnessPal, LoseIt!, Cal AI, Appediet) on 102 meals weighed to 0.1 g. The apps underestimated calories by about 250-345 kcal per meal and fat by about 30 g. They did worse on high-fat (keto) meals and best on carbohydrates. This is a preliminary conference abstract, not yet peer-reviewed. It directly supports the hidden-fat concern.
  src: https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/07/260726015237.htm (2026-07-26)

- Many nationwide (VPF) NIN dish cards also list ingredient grams, contrary to the summary's claim that they show kcal only. In my OCR of a 1-in-6 sample, 42 of 72 VPF cards list ingredient weights, often including vegetable oil, lard or butter (15 of the 42). Example: VPF-000127 khoai lang chiên lists 180 g sweet potato, 40 g flour, 20 g oil and 5 g butter, 574 kcal. Most VPF noodle-soup, bánh mì and snack cards (about VPF-000181 to 000349) show kcal only.
  src: https://viendinhduong.vn/storage/app/uploads/public/2025/12/12/screenshot_241.webp (2025-12-12)

- There is a Vietnam-specific precedent for an AI photo app grounded in NIN data. The IFPRI/NIN FRANI app was validated against weighed records in 36 adolescent girls in Thai Nguyen and came within 10% equivalence for energy, protein and fat. Nutrients came from the 2007 Vietnamese food composition table with cooking-retention factors. Gaps were filled from the 2019-2020 General Nutrition Survey composition database, plus Thai, ASEAN and USDA tables.
  src: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9535545/ (2022-08-09)

- Nutritionix allows caching only on the MVP and Unicorn plans, and even then only for users' food-log history. New lookups must still hit the API. This fills the researcher's gap. The page footer now reads © 2026 Syndigo LLC.
  src: https://www.nutritionix.com/api (accessed 2026-11-22)

- Edamam offers a free 'Food MCP' server on all Food Database plans. That is a direct way to let an LLM look up foods as a tool instead of relying on memory. Caching terms still apply.
  src: https://developer.edamam.com/food-database-api (accessed 2026-11-22)

- FatSecret lists MCP access and 'Custom Caching and Data Storage Permissions' on the Premier Free and Premier tiers, so the 24 h storage rule in its terms is only the default. Start-ups on Premier Free can get non-US datasets at a discount and image/NLP add-ons at 50% off.
  src: https://platform.fatsecret.com/api-editions (accessed 2026-11-22)
